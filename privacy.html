Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

Immich Google Photos Sync is a personal application used to synchronize selected photographs from a self-hosted Immich library to Google Photos.

Information Processed

The application processes selected photographs, associated file metadata, and the Google OAuth credentials necessary for authorized uploads.

Data Storage and Transfer

The synchronization software runs locally on a privately managed Synology NAS.

Selected photographs are uploaded to the Google Photos account authorized by the user.

OAuth credentials and synchronization records are stored locally and are not published on this website.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information, use advertising trackers, or independently share photographs with other third parties.

Uploaded photographs are processed and stored by Google Photos according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Access and Revocation

Google account authorization can be revoked through the user's Google Account settings.

Data Retention

Local staging files and synchronization records are retained on the NAS until removed by its operator. Revoking access does not automatically delete photographs previously uploaded to Google Photos.
